# Publications

Papers produced under, or closely associated with, the Squalomix Consortium, in reverse chronological order. Conference presentations and project milestones are listed in the chronology on the [About](about.md) page.

## 2026

- Kuraku, S. et al. Tracing genome size dynamics in sharks and rays with inclusive sequence analysis by the Squalomix Consortium. *Genome Research* 36: 1708–1723. [Article](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/36/8/1708) — first chromosome-scale genome assemblies of two batoid species (red stingray; whitebelly skate)
- Kawaguchi, Y. et al. Improved genome assembly of whale shark, the world's biggest fish. *GigaScience*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giag014)
- Teramura et al. Genome analysis of the silver chimaera. *DNA Research*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/dnares/dsag003)
- Yang et al. Genome analysis of the Greenland shark. *PNAS*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2601272123) (collaborative study)

## 2025

- Niwa, T. et al. Sex chromosome evolution in sharks and rays — the oldest sex chromosomes among vertebrates. *PNAS*. [DOI](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2513676122) — [NIG press release](https://www.nig.ac.jp/nig/2025/07/research-highlights/pr20250723.html)

## 2023

- Yamaguchi, K. et al. Elasmobranch genome sequencing reveals evolutionary trends of vertebrate karyotype organization. *Genome Research* 33: 1527–1540. [Article](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/33/9/1527) — first elasmobranch sex chromosome DNA sequences (zebra shark; whale shark)
- Unique 'blue-shift' mechanism of whale shark rhodopsin. *PNAS*. [DOI](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2220728120)
- Retained egg yolk protein (vitellogenin) repertoires in viviparous sharks. *Genome Biology and Evolution* 15: evad028. [Article](https://academic.oup.com/gbe/article/15/3/evad028/7048455)

## 2022

- Nishimura, O. et al. Squalomix: shark and ray genome analysis consortium and its data sharing platform. *F1000Research* 11: 1077. [Article](https://f1000research.com/articles/11-1077) — our position paper (version 2: 2025)

## 2020

- Uno, Y. et al. Cell culture and karyotyping of elasmobranch fishes. *Communications Biology* 3: 652. [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-020-01373-7)
