# About

## Leader

Squalomix is led by **Shigehiro Kuraku**, head of the [Molecular Life History Laboratory](https://www.treethinkers.info/squalomix) at the National Institute of Genetics (NIG), Mishima, Japan.

## Collaborations and societal contributions

- **Earth BioGenome Project (EBP)** — Squalomix is an [affiliated project](https://www.earthbiogenome.org/) of this global initiative to sequence life on Earth, since 2020.
- **European Reference Genome Atlas (ERGA)** — [partnership](https://www.erga-biodiversity.eu/) in the pan-European effort to produce reference genomes for biodiversity research, since 2025.
- **Shark-References** — [partnership](https://shark-references.com/) with the literature and taxonomy database for chondrichthyan research, since 2025.
- **Fisheries science and stock assessment (ICCAT / FRA)** — Through the Fisheries Research and Education Agency (FRA), Japan, we contribute to the Shark Research and Data Collection Programme (SRDCP) of the International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT), in particular its genome-wide genetic study of porbeagle stock structure and kinship, ongoing since 2022. The latest results were reported to the ICCAT Standing Committee on Research and Statistics (SCRS) as document SCRS/2026/214 (Ueda et al., 2026; see the [2026 SCRS Report](https://www.iccat.int/Documents/Meetings/Docs/2026/Reports/2026_SCRS_ENG.pdf)).
- **Japanese Red List Genome Project** — the Kuraku Lab contributes to this project, which catalogues the availability of genome sequences for endangered species in Japan ([project page](https://treethinkers.nig.ac.jp/redlist/)).
- **Aquariums and marine stations** — public aquariums and marine stations across Japan collaborate with us in sampling, giving access to rare and endangered species that are otherwise inaccessible, as in our long-standing work with Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium on the whale shark.

## Funding

The consortium is funded by intramural budgets granted by RIKEN and the National Institute of Genetics, Japan, as well as JSPS KAKENHI Grant Numbers 20H03269 and 16H06279 (PAGS), as stated in our [position paper](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.123591.1).

## 日本語による紹介

Squalomix（スクアロミクス）は、サメ・エイ・ギンザメなどの軟骨魚類を対象に、ゲノムをはじめとする分子レベルの情報を読み解き、その多様性と進化の理解を目指すコンソーシアムです。2020年に発足し、国立遺伝学研究所・分子生命史研究室（代表：Shigehiro Kuraku）が中心となって、水族館や臨海実験所、国内外の研究機関と連携しながら活動しています。染色体レベルのゲノム解読に、核型やゲノムサイズの実測、細胞培養による検証を組み合わせるのが特徴で、得られたデータと解析手法は順次公開しています。詳しくは [Home](index.md) と [History](history.md) をご覧ください。
